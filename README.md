# YES Full Circle Solutions — homepage redesign (preview)

Live preview: https://sami9188.github.io/yfc-homepage-preview/

The homepage follows the **YFC Doing Good To Do Good Website Brief (V4)**, Part C §6, sections 1–9, using its headings, copy, CTAs and header/footer guidance (Part B §5). The page leads with impact and stories, then explains how that good begins with responsible client action.

| # | V4 section | On the page |
|---|---|---|
| — | Header (§5) | Our Story · Impact and Stories · Solutions · Technology · Industries · Insights · More; CTA "Start Your Full Circle Journey"; compacts on scroll |
| 1 | Hero | #DoingGoodToDoGood, "Every responsible action can create a wider circle of good." Circle with six areas of good around it |
| 2 | Impact and Stories | Ocean Story, Mali water well, League of Extraordinary Schools (LACNOR); more stories; five indicators with explanations |
| 3 | How the Good Begins | Responsible action → operational value → a wider circle of good |
| 4 | What Doing Good to Do Good Means | Split circle: Doing Good / To Do Good |
| 5 | Solutions | Six pathways and a compact material explorer (14 categories) |
| 6 | The Full Circle Journey | Six steps, circular on desktop, swipeable on mobile |
| 7 | Create Good With Us | Schools, workplaces, communities; interactive tools (next phase) |
| 8 | Explore YFC | Technology, Industries, Trust and Recognition previews |
| 9 | Final CTA | "What Good Could We Create Together?" Form routed by enquiry type (solution, CSR, partnership, media) |
| — | Footer (§5) | "Close the Loop. Multiply the Good.", #DoingGoodToDoGood, newsletter, legal links |

## Files
- `index.html`, `css/styles.css`, `js/main.js`
- `assets/` — client imagery from the company profile, "Close the Loop" brochure and WRMEA cover story; `assets/story-*.jpg` are crops from the profile's impact page; `assets/proof/` holds certification and membership logos

## Design system
- Type: Inter Tight in The Digital Elites style (as requested).
- Colour: client "Green Lemons" palette (forest #013A20, emerald #478C5C, lint #BACC81, lemon #CDD193) with cream for warmth.
