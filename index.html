How it works

What happens after we collect

Every load follows the same loop through our facilities, under 24/7 camera surveillance and third-party safety audits.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Materials are collected and delivered to a YES Full Circle facility.

  2. 2

    Weigh & unpack

    Every load is weighed on arrival, so your reporting starts from a verified figure.

  3. 3

    Separate

    Recyclables are separated through physical, mechanical and electrical processes.

  4. 4

    Segregate

    Materials are sorted into containers by category.

  5. 5

    Shred & bale

    Plastic and metal packaging is shredded and turned back into raw material.

  6. 6

    Back to production

    Raw material goes to approved recyclers; residual material to an approved treatment facility.
