Environmental sponsorship

The Ocean Story

A two-year global photography project documenting how plastics and overfishing affect the ocean, with YFC as founding sponsor.

Read the full story
The need
Nine endangered marine species threatened by overfishing, ocean warming and plastic pollution.
YFC's role
Founding sponsor.
Partners
Photographer Vincent Kneefel.
Action
A two-year global project telling the stories of nine endangered marine species.
Outcome
Award-winning photographs, featured in the UN Decade of the Ocean Calendar 2022/2023. The project is now in its second edition.
Evidence
The published photographs and calendar feature.
Next step
Organisations can support future editions and awareness events.
