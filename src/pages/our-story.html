Why YFC exists

Why YFC Exists

YFC was created around a simple belief: responsible business can generate more than one kind of value. By helping organisations protect brands and manage products and materials responsibly, we can address immediate business needs while strengthening our ability to support a wider circle of environmental and social good.

This is #DoingGoodToDoGood. Doing Good describes the secure, responsible and measurable work we carry out with organisations. To Do Good describes how the strength created through our work helps us support meaningful environmental, educational, community and humanitarian initiatives.

Doing Good to Do Good is about generating social, environmental and financial value from waste that is traditionally seen as having no value.

What our name means

YES Stands for Your Environmental Savings.

Full Circle reflects our aim to prevent useful products and materials from reaching an unnecessary end point and to identify responsible pathways for recovery, reuse, recycling, upcycling or other suitable treatment.

Our work combines practical resource-recovery experience with technology, traceability and a social-enterprise mindset. We call this Doing Good to Do Good.

What guides us

Purpose, Vision and Mission.

Purpose

To make responsible action travel further, from secure material management to measurable environmental and social good.

Vision

A world where every organisation can close material loops and use responsible business to multiply positive impact.

Mission

To design and deliver secure, technology-enabled circular solutions that protect brands, recover resources, document outcomes and support meaningful environmental and social initiatives.

Our journey

Experience That Continues to Evolve.

  1. 2020

    YES Full Circle launches in Dubai.

    YFC opened its first facility in Al Quoz Industrial 3 in February 2020, built on the principle of Doing Good to Do Good. Weeks later the pandemic arrived; the team adapted and kept growing.

    More detail

    YFC runs as a for-profit business to keep financial discipline, while a substantial part of what it earns supports social and environmental initiatives.

  2. 2021

    Three new facilities and a first industry award.

    Facilities two, three and four opened, and YFC was named WEEE Recycling Company of the Year at the Middle East Waste & Recycling Awards.

    More detail

    The award recognised YFC as a WEEE processor, handling waste electrical and electronic equipment.

  3. 2022

    A fifth facility and recognition for responsible business.

    Facility five opened. YFC received Dubai Chamber CSR Labels for Workplace, Community and Environment, and the AmCham Dubai SME Award of Merit.

    More detail

    In 2022 alone, YFC recycled more than 19 million items, and its #DoingGoodToDoGood model was the cover story of Waste & Recycling Middle East.

  4. 2023

    Facility six opens in Jebel Ali.

    YFC's sixth facility opened in Jebel Ali, and YFC sponsored a delegate to the One Young World 2023 Summit in Belfast.

    More detail

    Wangechi Kuria attended One Young World as YFC's sponsored delegate. The summit brings together young leaders from around the world.

  5. 2024

    A third WEEE title and a Rising Star Award.

    YFC was named WEEE Recycling Company of the Year for the third time and received a Rising Star Award at the Middle East Waste & Recycling Awards.

    More detail

    In 2024, YFC received 5,137 tonnes of products and materials and recycled 98.7% of them (draft figures from the 2024 company profile).

  6. 2025

    New engagement programmes with brands.

    YFC launched a cosmetics recycling programme with the KIKO Milano team, connecting employees and customers with responsible recovery.

    More detail

    Brand programmes like this combine collection, secure handling and reporting with awareness for the people taking part.
