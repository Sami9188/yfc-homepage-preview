How it works

A Clear Journey From Requirement to Outcome.

Every solution follows the same five steps, shaped around your products, risks and goals.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We look at your products and materials, their condition, the risks and any requirements that apply.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We design a secure, responsible route and agree the logistics and documentation.

  3. 03

    Collection

    Products and materials are collected and transported to YFC facilities.

  4. 04

    Processing

    Debranding, destruction, segregation, recycling, upcycling or recovery, as the plan requires.

  5. 05

    Documentation

    Outcomes are documented and shared with you.

What you receive

  • Certificate
  • Mass balance
  • Reporting
  • Other applicable documentation
