With Sharaf DG

Book collection drive

Challenge
Used books, newspapers and catalogues ending up as waste.
Roles
Sharaf DG outlets collected donations; YFC recycled them.
Outcome
3,098 kg collected and sent for recycling.

With ACE

Tree-Cycle

Challenge
Real Christmas trees thrown away after the holidays.
Roles
ACE hosted drop-off points in its stores; YFC recycled the trees.
Outcome
The UAE's first circular-economy Christmas tree recycling programme.
