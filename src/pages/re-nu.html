What Re-Nu is

From Suitable Materials to New Possibilities.

Re-Nu explores how selected materials can be redesigned, repurposed or transformed into useful products and experiences.

Suitability depends on

  • Safety
  • Condition
  • Composition
  • Quantity
  • Intended use
  1. 1
    Select

    Recovered materials are assessed for safety, condition and composition.

  2. 2
    Redesign

    A design idea matches the material to a useful product or experience.

  3. 3
    Transform

    The material is cleaned, cut, shaped or reworked into something new.

  4. 4
    New story

    The finished product goes back into use, often carrying the story of where it came from.

Material to product

See What the Material Became.

Each story follows the material from what it was to what it became, and what changed along the way.

Recovered textilesBags and accessories

Suitable fabric is sorted and redesigned into bags, pouches and other textile products.

Discarded objectsHandicraft and décor

Records, frames and offcuts are reworked by hand into clocks and decorative pieces.

Sorted plasticsPlastic retail products

Plastic is sorted by type, shredded and shaped into colourful panels and coasters.

AluminiumIngot

Recovered aluminium is melted and formed into ingots that can return to manufacturing.

Organic materialCompost

Organic content separated from packaging is composted into material that helps new things grow.

Paper and cardboardCartons and boxes

Paper and cardboard are recycled into new cartons and boxes.
