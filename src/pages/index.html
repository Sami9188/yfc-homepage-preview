Good That Reaches Further.

YFC supports initiatives that protect the environment, expand access to learning and healthcare, strengthen communities and create opportunities. The strength created through responsible partnerships helps this good travel further.

Environmental protection

The Ocean Story

YFC is a founding sponsor of The Ocean Story, Vincent Kneefel's project telling the stories of nine endangered marine species threatened by overfishing, ocean warming and plastic pollution. The project is now in its second edition, and its photographs feature in the UN Decade of the Ocean Calendar 2022/2023.

Photo: Vincent Kneefel
Clean water

A water well in Mali

YFC sponsored the construction of a water well in Mali, Africa. The community now has access to clean water.

League of Extraordinary Schools

With LACNOR and the Dubai Health Authority, YFC reached 14 schools across Dubai, inspiring students to embrace environmental consciousness and healthier lives.

The Full Circle journey

Responsible Action Can Create More Than One Kind of Value.

Full circle

  1. Your organisation identifies a product, material, brand-protection or engagement need.

  2. YFC assesses the requirement and designs a secure, responsible route.

  3. Suitable products and materials follow the most appropriate available pathway.

  4. Applicable outcomes are documented through certificates, mass balance and reporting.

  5. Your organisation can communicate approved participation and outcomes.

  6. Responsible partnerships strengthen a wider circle of environmental and social good.
