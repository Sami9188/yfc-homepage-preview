What Full Circle means

Responsible action should never end at collection.

At YFC, Full Circle means looking beyond disposal. We protect products and brands, recover useful resources, document what happens next and use the strength created through our work to support a wider circle of environmental and social good.

Doing Good is how we help organisations protect products, recover resources and close material loops. To Do Good is how the value created through our work helps support environmental, educational and community initiatives. Together, they form one Full Circle of impact.

One Full Circle
  • Protect products, brands and confidential information
  • Segregate, recycle, recover and upcycle resources
  • Reduce avoidable disposal and support circularity
  • Document outcomes through certificates and reports
  • Support education and environmental awareness
  • Strengthen communities and meaningful initiatives
  • Encourage responsible behaviour and participation
  • Extend the value created through YFC's work

The Full Circle journey

One responsible action can travel further than you think.

Full circle

  1. Choose a responsible way to manage products and materials.

  2. We assess, plan, collect and manage the operational journey.

  3. Materials follow the best available recovery, recycling, upcycling or alternative pathway.

  4. Certificates, mass balance and reporting make the outcome visible.

  5. Your responsible action can be communicated and celebrated.

  6. The value created helps strengthen wider environmental and social initiatives.
